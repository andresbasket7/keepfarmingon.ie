PRIVACY POLICY

Keep Farming On (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website https://www.keepfarmingon.ie or our consultancy services.

1. Who we are

Keep Farming On is a farm consultancy service based in Ireland, focused on supporting family farms through generational renewal, succession planning, and farm family communication.

Contact us
Email: info@keepfarmingon.ie
Phone: 087 1655 555

2. What personal data we collect

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number.
  • Farm/family details: information you choose to share about your farm situation, family members, or succession goals.
  • Service records: notes from consultations, workshops, or coaching sessions.
  • Website data: information collected via our contact forms, cookies, or analytics tools (see Section 7).

We do not actively collect sensitive personal data unless you choose to provide it. We only use such information with your consent or where necessary to deliver our services.

3. How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide consultancy services.
  • To manage our professional relationship with you (e.g. scheduling sessions, invoicing).
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations.
  • To send updates, newsletters, or resources where you have given explicit consent.

4. Legal basis for processing

  • Contract: when we need your information to provide our consultancy services.
  • Consent: when you sign up for newsletters or provide optional details.
  • Legal obligation: when we are required to keep records for tax or regulatory purposes.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Enquiry forms: up to 12 months if you do not become a client.
  • Client records: for the duration of our consultancy relationship and up to 6 years afterwards.
  • Mailing list: until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent.

6. Sharing your data

We do not sell your data. We may share your data with trusted third parties, for example:

  • Website hosting and email providers.
  • Cloud storage providers (e.g. Google Workspace).
  • Accountants or professional advisors.

7. Cookies and website analytics

Our website may use cookies or analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

8. Your data protection rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Restrict or object to how we use your data.
  • Request transfer of your data to another provider.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise your rights, please contact us at info@keepfarmingon.ie.

9. Data security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to keep your data safe, including secure storage, encryption where appropriate, and access controls.

10. Data breaches

If a data breach occurs that risks your rights or freedoms, we will notify the Irish Data Protection Commission (DPC) within 72 hours and inform you if necessary.

11. Complaints

If you are concerned about how we handle your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission: https://www.dataprotection.ie

Updates to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be published on this page with an updated Last updated date.
