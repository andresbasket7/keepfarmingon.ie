INDIVIDUAL GUIDANCE

We are Family Farm SUCCESS SUPPORT and can help you

We provide Training, Mentoring, Coaching in communications, self-understanding and teamwork for your ongoing success.

What holds you back?

You will be less able to achieve your potential if you feel stuck, lack clarity, and lack energy. It will be difficult for you to grow if you are:

  • 🚫Off-focus
  • ❓Unsure where you are heading
  • ⏳Feeling the world could have passed me by!
  • ⚖️Suffer indecisiveness in decision-making
  • 🎤Unsure of the best approach in communication and discussions

We Facilitate Two Standard Programmes

Programme 1

Farmer Coaching Session

Two sessions · 2 hours each (25 years plus)

  1. A Complete a practical behavioural preference questionnaire following our initial discussion.
  2. B We use your feedback to gain fresh insights into your current challenges and opportunities.
  3. C A roadmap is developed to achieve both your immediate and long-term objectives.
  4. D Receive practical tools and support to sustain your ongoing change process.

These two sessions are ideal for farmers committed to self-directed professional development and serves as valuable preparation for career planning, interviews, and key meetings.

Programme 2

The Mentoring Programme

Six to ten sessions · 2–3 hours each (25 years plus)

  • Implement models for professional growth.
  • Make business growth repeatable and sustainable.
  • Resolve bottlenecks and breakdowns.
  • Boost creativity and innovation in leadership.
  • Bring clarity and know-how to your success factors.
  • Identify strategies tailored for you personally.

Includes 6 to 10 sessions (2–3 hours each) scheduled flexibly.

Ongoing support, practical tools, and questionnaire feedback to sustain development.
