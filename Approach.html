OUR APPROACH

Keepfarmingon, which evolved from Century Management and later Millennium Management International, is a firm of professional development consultants distinguished for its collaborative approach: LISTENING to customers/patrons, understanding in depth both the psychology of individuals and the evolving culture of the industry.

For decades, our mission has been to enable people, businesses, and farms to realize their FULL AND UNIQUE POTENTIAL, and to achieve ongoing fulfillment and lasting success.

Our approach views family farm intergenerational renewal not simply as a land-based transactional outcome, but as a transitional process. Years before the transfer, family members can engage in co-creation and contribute innovative ideas to make the family farm more sustainable and successful. This process allows the voices of the younger generation to be heard and helps unlock the full potential of family farm generational renewal , a secure base for for all as each transitions.

LISTENING also means continuously seeking out up to date information and engaging with those who are “in the know”: Teagasc, Vets, ACA, Marts, GPs, AA, Charter Accountants, Farmer Journal, Farm Exam, Farm Independent, Agriland.ie, Farmers Guardian, Farmers Weekly, FarmWeek.

SELF AND OTHER UNDERSTANDING


We promote practicable, applicable SELF UNDERSTANDING to enable empathy and open communication at family level for all members to participate and co-operate in understanding their role and/or supportive role in the future of sustainable family farm. For decades wehave used evidence –based, diagnostic approach including ‘state of the art’ evaluation tools and mediation ‘best-practice’ to understand and deal with the root causes of communications issues and stress.
Where appropriate we use tools such as validated psychometric tests to simplify the complicated and it is KEY to realise that different perception is not a personal decision.
Former IFA, COPA and Macra President , FBD Board Member, Padraig Walsh (Rip) said about our mentoring and training work, and was quoted by Maired Mc Guiness in the Farmer Journal "basically it is about understanding yourself first and understanding what makes others tick, sounds simple but it is an extremely complex subject".

Outcome

Behavioural research suggests that the most EFFECTIVE people are those who by using practical psychometric assessments understand themselves ( the useful and advantageous ratchet sockets) , both their strengths and weaknesses , so that they can develop strategies to meet the demands and opportunities of their lives and workplaces.
